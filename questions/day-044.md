# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Inventory Replenishment Alert based on Accelerating Sales Velocity

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at an e-commerce company specializing in electronics. The operations team is struggling to proactively manage inventory for fast-selling items, often leading to stockouts. They need a robust way to identify products that are selling faster than usual and whose current stock might not last long enough, so they can prioritize replenishment.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---

# Task
Identify products that meet the following criteria, indicating they need urgent replenishment:
1.  Their "recent sales velocity" (average daily sales quantity in the last 30 days, ending on `CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '1' DAY`) is greater than their "previous sales velocity" (average daily sales quantity in the 30 days *prior* to the recent 30 days).
2.  The increase in sales velocity (recent vs. previous) is greater than 10%.
3.  Based on their "recent sales velocity", their `current_stock_level` is projected to last for less than 7 days.

Calculate the `recent_avg_daily_sales`, `previous_avg_daily_sales`, `projected_days_of_stock`, and the `sales_velocity_increase_percentage` for each qualifying product. Order the results by `sales_velocity_increase_percentage` in descending order.

---
