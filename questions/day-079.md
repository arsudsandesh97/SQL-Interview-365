# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Potential Stale Inventory

## Business Scenario
An e-commerce company, "TrendGoods," is facing challenges in optimizing its warehouse space and managing inventory efficiently. As a Data Analyst, your task is to help the operations team identify products that might be sitting unsold for too long, thus tying up capital and warehouse resources. These "stale" products might be candidates for promotions, clearance sales, or strategic removal from inventory.

Specifically, the operations team wants to flag products that have a significant amount of stock but haven't seen any sales activity recently.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create products Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE products (
    product_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    product_name VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
    category VARCHAR(100),
    current_stock INT NOT NULL
);
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify products that have "Potential Stale Inventory". A product is considered to have "Potential Stale Inventory" if it meets both of the following criteria:
1. Its `current_stock` is greater than 10 units.
2. It has had *no sales* (quantity > 0 in `order_items`) in the last 60 days, calculated from the `CURRENT_DATE`.

The output should include `product_id`, `product_name`, `category`, and `current_stock` for these identified products, ordered by `product_id`.

---

| product_id | product_name           | category    | current_stock |
|------------|------------------------|-------------|---------------|
| P001       | Laptop Pro X           | Electronics | 15            |
| P004       | 27-inch Curved Monitor | Electronics | 12            |
