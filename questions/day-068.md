# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Subscription Customers

## Business Scenario
An e-commerce platform offers a tiered subscription service (Free, Standard, Premium) that provides benefits like faster shipping, exclusive discounts, and early access to sales. The Marketing Operations team wants to proactively identify "at-risk" customers to offer targeted retention campaigns. They define "at-risk" customers based on two criteria:

1.  **Recent Downgraders:** Customers who have downgraded their subscription tier (e.g., from Premium to Standard, Standard to Free, or Premium to Free) within the last 90 days.
2.  **Inactive Premium Subscribers:** Customers who currently hold an active 'Premium' subscription but have not placed any orders in the last 60 days.

Your task is to generate a report listing these "at-risk" customers along with specific details about their subscription status and last activity.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify "at-risk" customers based on the following criteria:

1.  **Recent Downgrader:** A customer who has an *active* subscription, and their immediately preceding *ended* subscription was of a higher tier than their current one, and the `end_date` of that preceding subscription was within the last 90 days from `CURRENT_DATE`.
2.  **Inactive Premium Subscriber:** A customer who currently has an *active* 'Premium' subscription (`end_date IS NULL`) but has not placed any orders in the last 60 days from `CURRENT_DATE` (or has never placed an order).

The output should include `customer_id`, `customer_status` (either 'Recent Downgrader' or 'Inactive Premium'), the `last_subscription_tier` (the tier immediately before their current one, if applicable, otherwise NULL), `current_subscription_tier`, and `last_order_date` (NULL if no orders).

Tier order for ranking: 'Free' < 'Standard' < 'Premium'.
