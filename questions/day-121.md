# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Post-Promotion Return Rate Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at an E-commerce company. The marketing department frequently runs various promotions to boost sales. However, there's a growing concern that some customers might be taking advantage of these promotions by purchasing items at a discounted price and then returning them shortly after the promotion ends. This behavior can lead to increased return processing costs and reduced profitability for promoted items.

The marketing team wants to identify products that are particularly susceptible to this "post-promotion return" phenomenon. Your task is to analyze sales data for a specific past promotion and calculate the percentage of units sold during that promotion that were subsequently returned *after* the promotion's official end date. This insight will help the team evaluate promotion effectiveness and refine future promotional strategies.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
For the promotion named 'Summer Flash Sale', calculate the "Post-Promotion Return Rate" for each product.
The "Post-Promotion Return Rate" for a product is defined as:
`(Total units of that product returned AFTER the promotion end date, from orders placed DURING the promotion) / (Total units of that product sold DURING the promotion) * 100`

Only consider orders placed strictly between the `start_date` and `end_date` (inclusive) of the 'Summer Flash Sale' promotion.
The result should include the `product_name`, `total_units_sold_during_promo`, `units_returned_post_promo`, and `post_promo_return_rate_percentage`.
Round the `post_promo_return_rate_percentage` to two decimal places.
Order the results by `post_promo_return_rate_percentage` in descending order.
If a product has no sales during the promo, it should not appear in the results. If a product has sales but no post-promo returns, its rate should be 0.00.
