# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Top Co-Purchased Product Pairs Analysis

## Business Scenario
An analytics team at "ElectroMart," a leading online electronics retailer, is tasked with optimizing product recommendations and cross-selling strategies. They want to understand which products are most frequently purchased *together within the same order* by customers. This insight will help them create effective "Customers Also Bought" sections on product pages, design promotional bundles, and optimize warehouse inventory layouts. Your goal is to identify the top product pairs that appear together in customer orders.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-27'
```

# Task
Identify the top 3 most frequently co-purchased *distinct* product pairs. A product pair (Product A, Product B) is considered co-purchased if both Product A and Product B appear in the same `order_id`. Ensure that each unique pair is counted only once, irrespective of the order of products (e.g., (Laptop Pro X, Wireless Mouse M1) should be treated the same as (Wireless Mouse M1, Laptop Pro X)).

The output should include:
1.  `product_name_1`: The name of the first product in the pair (alphabetically).
2.  `product_name_2`: The name of the second product in the pair (alphabetically).
3.  `co_purchase_count`: The total number of unique orders where this pair appeared together.

Order the results by `co_purchase_count` in descending order, then by `product_name_1` alphabetically, and then by `product_name_2` alphabetically.

---

# Expected Output Format
| product_name_1           | product_name_2          | co_purchase_count |
|--------------------------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Laptop Pro X             | Wireless Mouse M1       | 3                 |
| Mechanical Keyboard K3   | Wireless Mouse M1       | 3                 |
| Laptop Pro X             | Mechanical Keyboard K3  | 2                 |
