# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Proactive Stock-Out Risk Assessment for Trending Products

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at an e-commerce company specializing in electronics. The Operations and Inventory Management team is struggling with unexpected stockouts for popular products, leading to lost sales and customer dissatisfaction. They need a proactive mechanism to identify products that are trending upwards in sales velocity AND are at risk of running out of stock soon. Your task is to develop a SQL query that identifies these critical products to allow the team to prioritize replenishment.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify `product_id`, `product_name`, `current_stock_quantity`, `recent_daily_sales_avg`, `historical_daily_sales_avg`, and `estimated_days_of_supply_left` for products that meet the following criteria:

1.  **Significant Trend Upward:** The product's average daily sales over the **last 7 days** (up to `CURRENT_DATE - 1`) must be at least **20% higher** than its average daily sales over the **30 days immediately preceding** that 7-day period.
    *   *Recent Period:* `CURRENT_DATE - 7 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 1 day` (e.g., '2023-10-19' to '2023-10-25')
    *   *Historical Period:* `CURRENT_DATE - 37 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 8 days` (e.g., '2023-09-19' to '2023-10-18')
2.  **Imminent Stock-Out Risk:** Based on its `recent_daily_sales_avg`, the product has less than **14 days of supply** remaining.
    *   `estimated_days_of_supply_left` = `current_stock_quantity` / `recent_daily_sales_avg`.

Exclude any products that had zero sales in either the recent or historical period (to avoid division by zero or misleading trend calculations). Round `recent_daily_sales_avg`, `historical_daily_sales_avg`, and `estimated_days_of_supply_left` to two decimal places.
