# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Customer Attrition Risk Assessment Based on Purchase Behavior and Category Drift

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "TrendSetters," a thriving E-commerce company. The marketing team wants to proactively identify high-value customers who might be at risk of churning. They've hypothesized that customers who are high-spenders but haven't purchased recently, especially if their last purchase was in a product category they don't typically frequent, are prime candidates for targeted retention campaigns. Your task is to develop a SQL query to identify these "at-risk" high-value customers.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify "at-risk" high-value customers based on the following criteria:

1.  **High-Value Customer:** A customer whose `total lifetime spend` is greater than `$500.00`.
2.  **At-Risk Condition 1 (Recency):** Their `last_purchase_date` is older than `CURRENT_DATE - 90 days`.
3.  **At-Risk Condition 2 (Category Drift):** Their `last_purchase_category` is *not* among their top 2 most frequently purchased categories (measured by total `quantity` of items purchased in that category) over their entire purchase history.

The query should return `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `total_lifetime_spend`, `last_purchase_date`, `last_purchase_category`, `top_1_category`, and `top_2_category` for all identified customers.

---

# Expected Output Format
| customer_id | customer_name | total_lifetime_spend | last_purchase_date | last_purchase_category | top_1_category | top_2_category |
|-------------|---------------|----------------------|--------------------|------------------------|----------------|----------------|
| 2           | Bob Johnson   | 900.00               | 2023-06-15         | Books                  | Apparel        | Books          |
| 5           | Eve Adams     | 640.00               | 2023-05-20         | Food                   | Sports         | Food           |

*(Note: If a customer only has one top category by quantity, the `top_2_category` should be NULL or an empty string.)*
