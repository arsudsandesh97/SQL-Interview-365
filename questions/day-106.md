# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Underperforming Products by Category Rank Drop

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "TrendSphere," a leading e-commerce retailer. The Head of Category Management is concerned about potential "sleepers" – products that might be losing their market position within their respective categories, even if their absolute sales numbers aren't drastically low. She suspects that some products might be falling behind competitors or losing customer interest, which isn't immediately obvious from overall sales reports.

Your task is to identify products that have experienced a significant drop in their sales rank within their specific category when comparing their recent performance to their historical average performance. This insight will help the Category Manager prioritize products for strategic intervention, such as new marketing campaigns, pricing adjustments, or inventory review.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'
```

# Task
Identify products that have significantly dropped in their sales performance rank within their category.
To do this, you need to:
1.  Calculate the **total quantity sold** for each product within its category for two distinct periods:
    *   **Recent Period:** The last 30 days up to `CURRENT_DATE`.
    *   **Historical Period:** The 90 days immediately preceding the recent period.
2.  For both periods, assign a **rank** to each product based on its total quantity sold, *within its respective category*. The product with the highest quantity sold in a category gets rank 1. Use `RANK()`.
3.  Calculate the `rank_change` for each product by subtracting its `historical_rank` from its `recent_rank`. A positive `rank_change` indicates a drop in performance (e.g., going from rank 1 to rank 3 means a `rank_change` of 2).
4.  Filter for products where the `rank_change` is **greater than 1** (meaning it dropped at least 2 positions in rank, e.g., from 1st to 3rd, or 2nd to 4th, or even from 1st to 2nd if the change is strictly > 1).
5.  Return the `product_name`, `category_name`, `recent_quantity_sold`, `recent_rank`, `historical_quantity_sold`, `historical_rank`, and `rank_change`, ordered by `category_name` and then by `rank_change` in descending order.

**Note:** Only consider products that have sales data in *both* the recent and historical periods.
