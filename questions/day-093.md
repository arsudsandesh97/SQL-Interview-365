# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Critical High-Demand, Frequently Out-of-Stock Products

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a major E-commerce retailer. The Operations team is concerned about customer dissatisfaction and lost sales due to popular products being frequently out of stock, especially for high-value items. They suspect that some key products, despite high demand, are consistently facing inventory shortages. Your task is to identify these 'critical' products to help the supply chain and procurement teams prioritize their efforts and improve stock management.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify all 'critical' products that have been 'frequently out of stock' in the last 90 days leading up to `CURRENT_DATE`.

**Definitions:**
*   A product is considered 'critical' if its `category` is either 'Electronics' or 'Appliances'.
*   A product is considered 'frequently out of stock' if its `stock_level` was 0 for more than 20% of the days within the last 90-day period (inclusive of `CURRENT_DATE`).

For each such product, return its `product_name`, `category`, the total number of days it was out of stock within the last 90 days, and its total `quantity` sold within the same 90-day period.

Order the results by the number of out-of-stock days in descending order.

---

# Expected Output Format
| product_name            | category    | out_of_stock_days | total_quantity_sold_90_days |
|-------------------------|-------------|-------------------|-----------------------------|
| Laptop Pro X1           | Electronics | 30                | 4                           |
| Espresso Machine 3000   | Appliances  | 26                | 1                           |
