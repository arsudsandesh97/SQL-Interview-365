# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Monthly Plan-Specific Retention Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a growing SaaS company that offers tiered subscription plans (Free, Basic, Pro, Enterprise). The Product Management team wants to understand the effectiveness of each plan in retaining users. They are particularly interested in the **monthly retention rate for users who were active in a specific plan**, regardless of whether they upgraded, downgraded, or stayed on the same plan in the following month. This analysis will help identify which initial plans lead to better long-term engagement and inform marketing and product strategy.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-04-15'
```

```sql

    user_id VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,

    end_date DATE -- NULL indicates an active subscription with no known end date
```

# Task
Calculate the **monthly retention rate for each subscription plan**.
For each `month` (from the earliest subscription activity up to the month *prior* to `CURRENT_DATE`), identify:
1. The number of distinct `user_id`s who had an active subscription to *that specific `plan_name`* for *any part* of the given `month`. (This is the "initial cohort" for the month and plan).
2. The number of distinct `user_id`s from the initial cohort who were also active in *any* subscription plan for *any part* of the `month + 1`. (These are the "retained users").
3. The retention rate (retained users / initial cohort) as a percentage, rounded to two decimal places.

Consider a subscription active for a month if its `start_date` is on or before the end of the month AND its `end_date` (or `CURRENT_DATE` if `NULL`) is on or after the start of the month.

The output should include `analysis_month`, `plan_name`, `initial_cohort_users`, `retained_users`, and `retention_rate`. Order the results by `analysis_month` and then `plan_name`.
