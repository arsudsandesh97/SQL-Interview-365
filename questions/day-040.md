# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Feature Adoption to Premium Conversion

## Business Scenario
A SaaS company, "AnalyticsPro," offers various subscription plans including a "Free Trial" to onboard new users. They recently introduced an "Advanced Analytics" feature, initially available to trial users. The product team wants to evaluate the effectiveness of this new feature in driving upgrades to their highest tier, the "Premium" plan. As a Data Analyst, you need to identify users who actively engaged with the "Advanced Analytics" feature during their free trial and subsequently converted to a "Premium" plan within a specific timeframe after their trial ended.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-03-15'
```

---

# Database Setup

```sql
CREATE TABLE users (
    user_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    registration_date DATE
```

## Create subscriptions Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE subscriptions (
    subscription_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    user_id INT,
    plan_id INT,
    start_date DATE,
    end_date DATE, -- NULL if current active plan
    FOREIGN KEY (user_id) REFERENCES users(user_id),
    FOREIGN KEY (plan_id) REFERENCES plans(plan_id)
);
```

# Task
Identify users who meet all the following criteria:
1.  They had a 'Free Trial' plan whose `start_date` was between `2023-01-01` and `2023-01-31` (inclusive).
2.  They used the 'Advanced Analytics' feature *at least once* during their specific 'Free Trial' period (i.e., `usage_timestamp` falls between the `start_date` and `end_date` of that trial).
3.  They subsequently upgraded to a 'Premium' plan *within 30 days* of their 'Free Trial' `end_date`.

For these users, return their `user_id`, `registration_date`, the `start_date` of their 'Free Trial', the `end_date` of their 'Free Trial', and the `start_date` of their 'Premium' plan.
