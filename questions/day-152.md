# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Products with Frequent Early Returns by Same Customer

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a fast-growing e-commerce company. The Operations and Product teams are concerned about high return rates for certain products, which significantly impact profitability and customer satisfaction. They suspect that some products might have quality issues, misleading descriptions, or compatibility problems, leading customers to return the same item multiple times shortly after their initial purchase.

Your task is to identify products that exhibit this "suspicious" return pattern: a customer returning the *same product SKU* multiple times within a 30-day window from their *very first purchase date* of that specific SKU. This insight will help the teams prioritize investigations into product quality or marketing descriptions.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-04-01'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify `product_sku`s that have a "suspicious" return pattern. A product has a suspicious return pattern if, for any specific customer, they returned the *same product SKU more than once* where *all* these returns (for that product by that customer) occurred within 30 days of that customer's *very first purchase date* of that specific product SKU.

The query should return:
1.  `product_sku`: The SKU of the product identified.
2.  `total_suspicious_returns`: The total number of returns that contribute to this "suspicious" pattern across *all* customers for that `product_sku`.

Exclude any `product_sku` that does not exhibit this pattern for at least one customer.

---

# Expected Output Format

| product_sku | total_suspicious_returns |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| A101        | 4                        |
