# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Top Sequential Co-Purchases for Product Bundling

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company's product management team wants to improve product recommendations and potentially create new bundles. They are particularly interested in understanding customer purchasing behavior: specifically, which products are frequently bought *sequentially* by the *same customer* within a short time frame. This insight will help them identify strong co-purchase patterns that occur over time, rather than just in a single transaction, to suggest "buy next" products or create value-added bundles.

You, as a Data Analyst, are tasked with identifying the top 5 most common sequential product pairs, where the second product in the pair was purchased by the same customer within 24 hours of the first product.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify the top 5 most frequent sequential product pairs. A sequential product pair (Product A, Product B) occurs when a customer purchases Product B within 24 hours of purchasing Product A. For this analysis, consider each distinct `product_id` within an order at its `order_timestamp` as a purchase event. If a customer buys multiple products in the same order, they are considered to be purchased at the same `order_timestamp`. Order the output by `sequential_purchase_count` in descending order.

---

# Expected Output Format

| product_a_name     | product_b_name   | sequential_purchase_count |
| :----------------- | :--------------- | :------------------------ |
| Laptop             | Laptop Bag       | 3                         |
| External Monitor   | HDMI Cable       | 2                         |
| Coffee Mug         | Coffee Beans     | 2                         |
| Laptop             | Wireless Mouse   | 1                         |
| Wireless Mouse     | External Monitor | 1                         |
*(Note: For ties in sequential_purchase_count, any order of tied pairs is acceptable to break into the Top 5.)*
