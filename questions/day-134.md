# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Customer Churn-Reactivation Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "CloudPilot Inc.", a fast-growing SaaS company offering various subscription plans for their cloud management platform. Understanding customer lifecycle is crucial for strategic planning, especially identifying successful "win-back" strategies. Your team wants to analyze how often customers who churn (cancel their subscription) decide to reactivate their service shortly after. This insight will help in optimizing marketing efforts and improving customer retention strategies for previously churned users.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create Subscriptions Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Subscriptions (
    subscription_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_id INT NOT NULL,
    plan_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    start_date DATE NOT NULL,
    end_date DATE -- NULL if the subscription is currently active or future-dated
);
```

# Task
Identify all instances where a customer who previously ended a subscription (`end_date` is not NULL) subsequently reactivated (started a new subscription) within 90 days of their *previous subscription's end date*.

For each such reactivation, calculate the duration (in days) the customer was churned.
Exclude immediate renewals/upgrades where `churn_duration_days` is 0.

The results should be ordered by `customer_id` and then `reactivation_start_date`.
