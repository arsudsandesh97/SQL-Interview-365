# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Customer Subscription Lifecycle Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a fast-growing SaaS company. The marketing and customer success teams are keen to understand customer loyalty and true active subscription durations. They've observed that customers sometimes cancel their subscriptions but resubscribe shortly after. They want to define a "subscription cycle" as a continuous period of active subscription. If a customer resubscribes within 30 days of their previous subscription ending, it's considered part of the same cycle. If the gap is longer than 30 days, it's treated as a new, distinct subscription cycle. Your task is to analyze the raw subscription data to identify these cycles and calculate their total duration in days.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create customer_subscriptions Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE customer_subscriptions (
    subscription_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_id INT,
    plan_type VARCHAR(50),
    start_date DATE,
    end_date DATE NULL -- NULL if the subscription is currently active
);
```

# Task
Identify distinct subscription cycles for each customer based on the following rule:
A new subscription is considered part of an existing cycle if its `start_date` is within 30 days (inclusive) of the `end_date` of the customer's *immediately preceding* subscription. Otherwise, it marks the beginning of a new cycle. For subscriptions that are currently active (i.e., `end_date` is NULL), use `CURRENT_DATE` as their effective `end_date` for cycle duration calculation.

Calculate the `cycle_start_date`, `cycle_end_date`, and the `total_cycle_days` (inclusive of start and end dates) for each identified subscription cycle.
