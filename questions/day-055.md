# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Subscription Renewal Failure Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "SubBox HQ", a growing e-commerce company specializing in subscription boxes. The customer retention team is struggling to prioritize their outreach efforts for customers whose subscription renewals fail. They need a way to quickly identify customers who recently experienced a failed renewal and understand their prior engagement. Specifically, they want to know, for each subscription with a *recent failed renewal*, how many *consecutive successful payments* the customer made immediately *prior* to that failure, and the total revenue generated from those specific successful payments. This insight will help them tailor their recovery strategy – for instance, a customer with a long successful streak might just need a payment method update, while one with zero prior successes might indicate a deeper satisfaction issue.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2024-03-15'
```

# Database Setup

```sql
CREATE TABLE customers (
```

## Create transactions Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE transactions (
    transaction_id VARCHAR(10) PRIMARY KEY,
    subscription_id VARCHAR(10),
    customer_id INT,
    transaction_date DATE,
    amount DECIMAL(10, 2),
    status VARCHAR(20) -- 'SUCCESS', 'FAILED', 'PENDING'
);
```

# Task
For each `subscription_id` where the *latest* transaction has a `status` of 'FAILED', identify the customer and calculate:
1.  The `last_failed_date` of that subscription's latest failed transaction.
2.  The `successful_streak_count`: The number of *consecutive successful* transactions that occurred immediately *prior* to this latest failed transaction.
3.  The `successful_streak_value`: The sum of `amount` for those consecutive successful transactions.

If there are no successful transactions immediately prior to the latest failed one, the `successful_streak_count` and `successful_streak_value` should be 0.

Order the results by `customer_id` ascending, then `subscription_id` ascending.
