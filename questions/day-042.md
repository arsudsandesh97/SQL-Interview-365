# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identify Underperforming High-Stock Products for Promotion

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "GourmetGoods," an e-commerce platform specializing in premium food products. The marketing team is planning a major promotional campaign for the upcoming holiday season. They want to identify products that are currently "underperforming" relative to their category peers but have "significant inventory" that needs to be moved. The goal is to prioritize these products for discounts or bundle offers to clear stock and boost sales, especially for items that might be collecting dust.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create Products Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Products (
    product_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    product_name VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    category VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    current_stock INT NOT NULL
);
```

# Task
Identify products that are candidates for promotional offers or markdowns based on the following criteria:

1.  **Underperforming Sales:** The product's total sales quantity in the last 30 days (up to and including `CURRENT_DATE`) must be less than 50% of the *average total sales quantity for all products within its same category* over the same 30-day period. Products with zero sales in the period should be considered to have 0 quantity for comparison.
2.  **Significant Inventory:** The product must currently have more than 100 units in stock (`current_stock > 100`).

Return the `product_id`, `product_name`, `category`, `current_stock`, the `recent_sales_quantity` for that product, and the `category_average_sales_quantity` for its category (rounded to 2 decimal places). Order the results by `product_id`.
