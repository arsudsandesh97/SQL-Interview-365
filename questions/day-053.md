# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Customers Based on Purchase Frequency Shift

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company wants to proactively identify "at-risk" customers to implement targeted re-engagement campaigns. The marketing team believes that customers who have significantly slowed down their purchasing frequency recently, compared to their historical buying patterns, and who haven't made a purchase in a while, are prime candidates for intervention. Your role as a Data Analyst is to identify these customers using SQL.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---

```sql
CREATE TABLE orders (

    customer_id INT NOT NULL,
    order_date DATE NOT NULL,
    total_amount DECIMAL(10, 2) NOT NULL
```

# Task
Identify customers who meet ALL of the following criteria as "at-risk":

1.  **Sufficient History:** The customer has made at least 5 orders in total.
2.  **Recent Engagement Drop:** Their average time (in days) between purchases for their *last 3 orders* is at least 1.5 times their *overall average time between purchases*. (For calculating time between purchases, consider intervals between consecutive orders. The first order for a customer has no preceding order, so it doesn't contribute to an interval.)
3.  **Inactivity:** Their last purchase date was more than 30 days before `CURRENT_DATE`.

Return the `customer_id`, their `last_purchase_date`, their `overall_avg_days_between_purchases`, and their `recent_avg_days_between_purchases`. Order the results by `customer_id`. Round average days to 2 decimal places.
