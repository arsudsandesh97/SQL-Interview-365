# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Category-Specific Customer Disengagement

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "ShopSavvy", a rapidly growing e-commerce company. The Marketing team is concerned about customer churn, but more specifically, they want to identify customers who might be disengaging from *certain product categories* they previously bought from, even if they are still making purchases in other categories. This "category-specific disengagement" indicates a potential shift in preference or a competitor gaining traction in that specific area, and they want to re-engage these customers with targeted promotions.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'
```

# Task
Identify customers who exhibit "category-specific disengagement". A customer is considered disengaged from a specific product category if *all* of the following conditions are met:
1.  They have made at least **2 separate purchases** from that specific category in their purchase history. (This indicates prior engagement).
2.  Their **last purchase date** from that specific category was **more than 90 days ago** (relative to `CURRENT_DATE`).
3.  They have made **at least one purchase from *any* category** within the last **30 days** (relative to `CURRENT_DATE`). (This filters out entirely inactive customers and focuses on those still active on the platform but disengaging from a specific category).

Your query should return the `customer_name`, `customer_email`, the `category_name` they are disengaging from, the `last_purchase_date_in_category`, and the `total_purchases_in_category`.
Order the results by customer name and then by category name.
