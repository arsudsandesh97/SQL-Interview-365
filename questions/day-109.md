# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Premium Customer Loyalty Streak Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a growing E-commerce company that offers a "Premium Membership" service. This membership provides various benefits and is crucial for customer retention. The marketing team wants to understand the true loyalty of premium members by identifying how many *consecutive months* they actively engage with the platform *after their initial premium signup month*. An active engagement month is defined as a month where a customer has an active premium subscription AND makes at least one purchase.

The goal is to measure the longest such streak for each customer, excluding the very first month they became a premium member.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-12-01'
```

# Task
Your task is to identify the *maximum number of consecutive months* a premium member maintained an 'Active Premium with Purchase' status *after their initial premium signup month*.

An 'Active Premium with Purchase' month is defined as a calendar month where:
1.  The customer had an active premium subscription.
2.  The customer made at least one purchase.

You need to return the `customer_id` and their `max_consecutive_active_months_after_initial`.
Exclude any customers who did not have at least one 'Active Premium with Purchase' month *after* their initial premium signup month.
Consider all months up to the month *prior* to `CURRENT_DATE`. So, if `CURRENT_DATE` is `2023-12-01`, consider months up to November 2023.

---

|-------------|---------------------------------------------|
