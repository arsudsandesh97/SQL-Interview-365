# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk E-commerce Customers Based on Purchase Frequency

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company's Marketing team wants to proactively identify "at-risk" customers to launch re-engagement campaigns. As a Data Analyst, your task is to identify customers who have significantly deviated from their usual purchase patterns, suggesting they might churn soon. This involves analyzing their historical purchase frequency and recent activity relative to a defined "at-risk" threshold.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-31'
```

# Task
Identify all "at-risk" customers according to the following criteria:
1.  The customer has made at least two purchases in total (to establish a purchase frequency).
2.  Their `days_since_last_purchase` (calculated from `CURRENT_DATE`) is strictly greater than 30 days.
3.  Their `days_since_last_purchase` is also strictly greater than `1.75` times their historical `average_days_between_purchases`.
    *   `average_days_between_purchases` is the average time (in days) between consecutive orders for that customer.

Your output should include `customer_id`, their `last_purchase_date`, their `days_since_last_purchase`, and their `avg_days_between_purchases`. Round `avg_days_between_purchases` to 2 decimal places. Order the results by `customer_id`.
