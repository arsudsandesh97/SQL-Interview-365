# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Product Sales Velocity Shift Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "GadgetGrove," a rapidly growing e-commerce retailer specializing in electronics. The operations and marketing teams need to proactively manage inventory and adjust campaigns based on product performance shifts. They've identified that products experiencing a sudden surge or sharp decline in sales velocity require immediate attention for either potential re-stocking/promotion or clearance/markdown strategies.

Your task is to identify products that have had a significant percentage change in sales velocity over the last 30 days compared to their typical historical performance.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify products with significant shifts in sales velocity. Calculate the percentage change in sales velocity for each product using the following rules:
1.  **Recent Sales Velocity**: Total `quantity` sold for the product in the last 30 full days immediately preceding `CURRENT_DATE`. (i.e., `order_date` from '2023-09-26' to '2023-10-25').
2.  **Historical Sales Velocity**: The *average daily quantity* sold for the product over the 90 full days immediately preceding the Recent Sales Period, scaled to 30 days for comparison. (i.e., `order_date` from '2023-06-28' to '2023-09-25'). Formula: `(Total quantity sold in historical 90 days / 90) * 30`.
3.  **Percentage Change**: `((Recent Sales Velocity - Historical Sales Velocity) / Historical Sales Velocity) * 100`.
    *   If `Historical Sales Velocity` is 0 but `Recent Sales Velocity` is greater than 0, represent the percentage change as `99999.99` (indicating infinite growth).
    *   If both `Historical Sales Velocity` and `Recent Sales Velocity` are 0, represent the percentage change as `0.00`.
4.  **Filtering**: Include only products that have sold at least **10 units** in the Recent Sales Period (`Recent Sales Velocity >= 10`).
5.  **Output**: Return products where the absolute `Percentage Change` is greater than 50% (i.e., `Percentage Change > 50` or `Percentage Change < -50`).
6.  Order the results by `sales_velocity_change_percent` in descending order.
7.  Format `sales_velocity_change_percent` to two decimal places.
