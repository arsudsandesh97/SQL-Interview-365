# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Recently Inactive Power Users for Re-engagement

## Business Scenario
A leading SaaS company offering a project management platform is concerned about user churn. As a Data Analyst, you've been tasked by the Growth team to identify 'Recently Inactive Power Users'. These are high-value users who exhibited significant engagement in the past but have recently stopped using the platform. The goal is to proactively reach out to these users with targeted re-engagement campaigns.

The business defines a 'Power User Trait' as a user who had at least one continuous streak of 5 or more consecutive daily logins. This streak must have occurred entirely within the 60-day period ending on `CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '8' DAY` (inclusive). Specifically, all login dates within the qualifying streak must be greater than or equal to `CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '68' DAY` and less than or equal to `CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '8' DAY`.

'Recently Inactive' is defined as a user whose most recent login (across all history) was strictly more than 7 days ago relative to `CURRENT_DATE` (i.e., their last login date is earlier than `CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '7' DAY`).

Your task is to provide a list of `user_id`s who fit this description.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```
