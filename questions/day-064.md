# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying High-Risk Return Customers

## Business Scenario
A rapidly growing e-commerce company is experiencing challenges with high return rates, which impact logistics, inventory management, and overall profitability. The head of Operations suspects that a subset of customers might be contributing disproportionately to these issues by frequently returning items and then making new purchases that also end up being returned at a high rate. This behavior could indicate "wardrobing" (buying, using, returning) or simply an inconsistent purchasing pattern that strains resources.

As a Data Analyst, you are tasked with identifying these "High-Risk Return Customers" to help the operations and marketing teams develop targeted strategies (e.g., tailored recommendations, adjusted return policies, or deeper customer support engagement) to mitigate future losses.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify `customer_id`s and `customer_name`s of "High-Risk Return Customers". A customer is considered "High-Risk" if ALL of the following conditions are met:

1.  **High Volume of Returns:** The customer has returned at least 3 distinct items (where `item_status = 'RETURNED'`).
2.  **Frequent Post-Return Purchases:** For *at least one* of their returned items, the customer made a *new purchase* (any item with `item_status = 'PURCHASED'`) within 30 days *after* the `return_date` of that specific returned item.
3.  **High Return Rate on Subsequent Purchases:** Among *all unique items* that were purchased by the customer within 30 days *after any* of their return dates (i.e., combining all purchases that fall into such 30-day windows across all their returns), the overall return rate for these specific subsequent purchases is strictly greater than 40%. The return rate is calculated as (Number of returned items from this subset / Total number of purchased items in this subset).
