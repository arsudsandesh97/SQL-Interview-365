# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Analyzing Lapsed-and-Retained Customer Subscriptions

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at 'SubscribrCo', a popular SaaS company offering various subscription plans. The marketing team is trying to understand customer retention better. Specifically, they are interested in 'lapsed-and-retained' customers. These are customers who allowed their subscription to expire (i.e., did not renew immediately on the day their previous subscription ended) but then *resubscribed within 30 days* of their previous subscription's end date. This group is distinct from continuously subscribed customers and completely churned customers. The team wants to analyze how long these customers remained lapsed before resubscribing, to potentially target them with specific re-engagement campaigns.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Database Setup

```sql
CREATE TABLE customer_subscriptions (

    customer_id INT NOT NULL,
```

# Task
Identify all instances where a customer was 'lapsed-and-retained'. For each such instance, report the `customer_id`, the `end_date` of their previous subscription, the `start_date` of their subsequent (retained) subscription, and the number of `days_lapsed`.

`days_lapsed` is defined as the number of days between the end of the previous subscription and the start of the next one (i.e., `next_start_date - previous_end_date`).

Apply the following conditions:
1. Exclude customers who renewed continuously (i.e., `next_start_date` is the day immediately following `previous_end_date`). This means `days_lapsed` must be greater than 1.
2. Exclude customers who resubscribed after more than 30 days. This means `days_lapsed` must be less than or equal to 30.

Order the results by `customer_id` and then `previous_subscription_end_date`.
