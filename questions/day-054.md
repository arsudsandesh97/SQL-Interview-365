# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: E-commerce: Identifying Frequently Paired Product Returns

## Business Scenario
An e-commerce company's Operations team has noticed an increasing trend in product returns. They suspect that certain combinations of products might be frequently returned together by the same customers, possibly indicating compatibility issues, misleading product descriptions, or customer expectations mismatches when these items are purchased simultaneously or sequentially.

Your role as a Data Analyst is to identify these "paired return" patterns. Specifically, the team wants to know which pairs of products (Product A, Product B) are most often returned by the *same unique customer*, where both products in the pair were returned within 30 days of their *individual purchase dates*.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify pairs of products (Product A, Product B) such that for a given customer, *both* Product A and Product B were purchased and subsequently returned within a 30-day window from their *respective purchase dates*.

Your query should return the `product_name_a`, `product_name_b`, and the `unique_customer_pair_return_count`.
- `product_name_a` should always be alphabetically before `product_name_b` to avoid duplicate pairs (e.g., 'A, B' but not 'B, A').
- The `unique_customer_pair_return_count` is the number of distinct customers who returned *both* products within the specified criteria.
- Order the final result by `unique_customer_pair_return_count` in descending order, then by `product_name_a` ascending, then `product_name_b` ascending.

---

# Expected Output Format
| product_name_a     | product_name_b     | unique_customer_pair_return_count |
|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Ergonomic Keyboard | Monitor Stand      | 2                                 |
| Laptop Charger     | Ergonomic Keyboard | 1                                 |
