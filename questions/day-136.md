# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Co-Purchase Revenue Impact Analysis for a Key Product

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "GadgetHub," a growing e-commerce company specializing in electronics. The marketing team is planning a new campaign around their popular 'Wireless Headphones' and wants to understand its co-purchase behavior. Specifically, they want to identify which *other* product is most frequently bought *alongside* 'Wireless Headphones' within the same order. Once identified, they want to know the total revenue generated *specifically from orders where both* 'Wireless Headphones' and this most frequently co-purchased product were present. This insight will help them strategize product bundles or "customers also bought" recommendations to maximize revenue.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify the product (let's call it the `Anchor_Product`) that is most frequently purchased together with 'Wireless Headphones' within the same order. Then, calculate the total revenue generated from *all items* in orders where *both* 'Wireless Headphones' and this identified `Anchor_Product` were present.

Your output should include:
1.  The `product_name` of 'Wireless Headphones'.
2.  The `product_name` of the most frequently co-purchased `Anchor_Product`.
3.  The `total_revenue` from all orders containing both products.

If there's a tie for the most frequently co-purchased product, pick any one.

---

# Expected Output Format
| target_product_name | anchor_product_name | total_revenue |
|---------------------|---------------------|---------------|
| Wireless Headphones | Charging Cable      | 575.00        |
