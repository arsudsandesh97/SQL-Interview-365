# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: E-commerce Subscription Reactivation Analysis

## Business Scenario
A leading E-commerce subscription box company wants to better understand customer loyalty and re-engagement patterns. Specifically, they've identified that a customer is considered to have "churned" if there's a gap of *more than 60 days* between the end of one subscription period and the start of a subsequent one. Conversely, a "reactivation" occurs when a customer starts a new subscription *after* a period of inactivity that met the "churn" definition.

As a Data Analyst, your task is to identify all instances of customer reactivation and quantify how long each customer was inactive before reactivating their subscription. This analysis will help the marketing team understand the effectiveness of win-back campaigns and optimize customer retention strategies.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE subscriptions (
    subscription_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_id INT,
```

# Task
Write a SQL query that identifies all customer reactivations. For each reactivation, calculate the number of days the customer was inactive before starting their new subscription.
A reactivation is defined as a new subscription starting after a gap of more than 60 days from the `end_date` of their previous subscription.

The query should return the following columns:
1.  `customer_id`
2.  `reactivation_subscription_id` (the `subscription_id` of the reactivated subscription)
3.  `reactivation_start_date` (the `start_date` of the reactivated subscription)
4.  `inactive_duration_days` (the number of days between the `end_date` of the previous subscription and the `start_date` of the reactivated subscription)

Order the results by `customer_id` and then `reactivation_start_date`.
