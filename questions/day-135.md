# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Customer Subscription Migration Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "TierUp Subscriptions," an E-commerce company offering different product subscription tiers (Basic, Premium, Gold). The Head of Customer Success wants to understand customer migration patterns between subscription tiers for Q1 2023 to identify growth areas, potential churn risks, and customer loyalty. Specifically, they want to analyze the change in subscription status for customers who were active at the beginning of Q1 2023 by the end of the quarter.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-04-01'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create customer_subscriptions Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE customer_subscriptions (
    subscription_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_id INT,
    tier VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    start_date DATE NOT NULL,
    end_date DATE -- NULL if the subscription is still active/ongoing
);
```

# Task
Analyze customer subscription migration over Q1 2023 (January 1st, 2023 to March 31st, 2023).

For *all customers who had an active subscription on January 1st, 2023*, determine their subscription status change by March 31st, 2023.

Classify each customer's migration into one of the following categories:
- **'Upgraded'**: If their tier on March 31st, 2023 is higher than their tier on January 1st, 2023. (Tier hierarchy: Basic < Premium < Gold)
- **'Downgraded'**: If their tier on March 31st, 2023 is lower than their tier on January 1st, 2023.
- **'Maintained'**: If their tier on March 31st, 2023 is the same as their tier on January 1st, 2023.
- **'Churned'**: If they had an active subscription on January 1st, 2023 but no active subscription on March 31st, 2023.

Consider a subscription as active on a given `target_date` if `start_date <= target_date` AND (`end_date IS NULL` OR `end_date > target_date`). If a customer has multiple valid subscriptions on a `target_date`, consider the one with the latest `start_date` as their current tier.

Your output should include `customer_id`, their `start_tier_q1`, their `end_tier_q1`, and their `migration_status`.
