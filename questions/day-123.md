# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Dormant Active Subscribers for Churn Risk

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "CloudConnect," a SaaS company offering various subscription plans. The Customer Success team wants to proactively identify customers who might be at risk of churning. They hypothesize that customers who have an "Active" subscription but haven't used the product recently (i.e., are "dormant") are prime candidates for proactive outreach. Your task is to generate a report listing these "dormant active" subscribers, including their details and the last time they interacted with the product.

A customer is considered "dormant active" if:
1.  Their current subscription status is 'active'.
2.  Their subscription `end_date` is in the future relative to `CURRENT_DATE`.
3.  Their last recorded `activity_timestamp` (if any exists) was more than 30 days prior to `CURRENT_DATE`.
4.  OR, if they have *no* recorded activity, their subscription `start_date` was more than 30 days prior to `CURRENT_DATE`.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```


```sql
CREATE TABLE subscriptions (
    subscription_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_id VARCHAR(50),
    plan_name VARCHAR(100),
    start_date DATE,
    end_date DATE,
    status VARCHAR(50), -- 'active', 'cancelled', 'expired'
    FOREIGN KEY (customer_id) REFERENCES customers(customer_id)
);
```

# Task
Write a SQL query that identifies all "dormant active" subscribers based on the definition provided in the business scenario, using `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`.

The query should return the following columns:
`customer_name`, `email`, `subscription_id`, `plan_name`, `subscription_start_date`, `subscription_end_date`, `last_activity_timestamp` (NULL if no activity was ever recorded for the customer).
Order the results by `customer_name`.
