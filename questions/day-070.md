# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Problematic Delivery Routes

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at SpeedyShip, a large e-commerce logistics company. The Operations team is concerned about customer satisfaction due to missed delivery windows. They've observed an increase in customer complaints related to late deliveries. Your task is to identify specific delivery routes (defined by a combination of `route_id` and `driver_id`) that are consistently underperforming based on recent activity. This analysis will help them prioritize which routes/drivers need intervention, such as re-training, route optimization, or closer monitoring.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

    actual_delivery_time DATETIME,

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify `route_id` and `driver_id` combinations that meet the following criteria based on deliveries completed in the last 30 days (inclusive of `CURRENT_DATE - 1`):
1.  The 'late delivery rate' (percentage of total deliveries that were late) for that route/driver combination exceeds **20%**. A delivery is considered late if `actual_delivery_time` is strictly later than `scheduled_delivery_time`.
2.  The total number of deliveries for that route/driver combination in the specified period is at least **10**.

Your output should include `route_id`, `driver_id`, `total_deliveries`, and `late_delivery_rate` (as a percentage, rounded to two decimal places, e.g., 25.00 for 25%). The results should be ordered by `late_delivery_rate` in descending order.

---

# Expected Output Format

| route_id | driver_id | total_deliveries | late_delivery_rate |
| :------- | :-------- | :--------------- | :----------------- |
| R-C      | DRV-3     | 12               | 25.00              |
