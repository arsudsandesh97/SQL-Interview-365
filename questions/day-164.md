# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: E-commerce Product Subscription Churn Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst for a rapidly growing e-commerce platform that offers various products on a subscription basis. Understanding customer churn at the product level is critical for identifying product performance issues, optimizing marketing campaigns, and improving retention strategies. Your task is to identify unique customers who have churned from specific product subscriptions during a target month, considering various subscription states and potential re-subscriptions. This analysis will help the product and marketing teams focus their efforts.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'
```

    FOREIGN KEY (product_id) REFERENCES products(product_id)
);

# Task
Identify unique customers who churned from a *specific product subscription* during the month of **October 2023**.

A customer is considered to have churned on a product in October 2023 if *all* the following conditions are met:

1.  **Prior Activity:** The customer had at least one subscription for `product_id` that was `ACTIVE` or `PAUSED` at the *beginning* of October 2023 (i.e., `start_date` <= '2023-10-01' AND (`end_date` IS NULL OR `end_date` >= '2023-10-01')).
2.  **Termination during Month:** *All* of their subscriptions for that `product_id` that meet condition 1 either:
    *   Had their `end_date` fall *within* October 2023 (i.e., '2023-10-01' <= `end_date` <= '2023-10-31').
    *   *If `end_date` was initially NULL*, the `status` changed to `cancelled` or `expired` with an implied `end_date` (or effective cancellation date) within October 2023. (For simplicity, assume `end_date` is updated upon cancellation/expiration).
3.  **No Immediate Re-subscription/Continuation:** The customer did *not* initiate any *new* `ACTIVE` subscription for the *same `product_id`* that commenced *within* October 2023 (`start_date` between '2023-10-01' and '2023-10-31' inclusive) and extended *beyond* October 2023 (i.e., `end_date` is NULL or `end_date` >= '2023-11-01'). This condition prevents counting "renewals" or seamless re-subscriptions as churn.

The output should include the churn month, customer details, and product details for each churn event.
