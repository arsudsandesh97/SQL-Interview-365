# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying "At-Risk" Subscribers in a Coffee Subscription Service

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "BeanBox," a gourmet coffee subscription service. The marketing team is concerned about potential customer churn and wants to proactively identify subscribers who show signs of disengagement. They're particularly interested in subscribers who have recently paused their service and also seem to be struggling to find their preferred coffee, indicated by multiple recent changes in their coffee preferences. Your task is to provide a list of such "at-risk" subscribers for targeted re-engagement campaigns.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2024-03-01'
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE subscribers (

    signup_date DATE,
```

# Task
Identify all "At-Risk" subscribers who satisfy the following three conditions based on `CURRENT_DATE = '2024-03-01'`:

1.  **Recent Pause:** The subscriber has paused their subscription (`action_type = 'PAUSE'`) at least once in the *last 3 months* (inclusive of `CURRENT_DATE`). This means on or after `2023-12-01`.
2.  **Frequent Preference Changes:** The subscriber has made at least two *distinct* coffee preference changes (where `old_preference` is different from `new_preference`) within the *last 6 months* (inclusive of `CURRENT_DATE`). This means on or after `2023-09-01`.
3.  **Active/Paused Status:** The subscriber's `current_status` is not 'CANCELLED'.

The output should include the `subscriber_id`, `email`, `signup_date`, `initial_coffee_preference`, the `last_pause_date` (most recent pause within the 3-month window), and `num_preference_changes_6m` (count of distinct preference changes in the 6-month window).
