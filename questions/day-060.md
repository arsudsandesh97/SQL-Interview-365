# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Churn Risk: Pre-Churn Product Engagement Breadth Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "Subscribely," an e-commerce company offering subscription boxes for various product categories. Your team is focused on understanding customer churn and identifying early warning signs. The product team suspects that customers might reduce their engagement or narrow their product interests in the period leading up to their churn. You've been asked to investigate this hypothesis by analyzing the diversity of products customers interact with before they cancel their subscriptions.

Specifically, you need to identify customers who churned in Q1 2023 and compare their distinct product engagement breadth in two specific windows prior to their churn.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-04-01'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create Customers Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    join_date DATE NOT NULL,
    subscription_status VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- 'active', 'inactive'
    churn_date DATE -- NULL if active
);
```

# Task
Your task is to identify all customers who churned in Q1 2023 (January 1st, 2023 to March 31st, 2023). For these churned customers, calculate the number of *distinct products* they interacted with in two specific periods prior to their churn:

1.  **Window A:** The 30-day period immediately *before* their `churn_date`.
2.  **Window B:** The 30-day period *before* Window A (i.e., 31 to 60 days before their `churn_date`).

The final output should show each churned customer, their `churn_date`, the count of distinct products interacted with in Window A, and the count of distinct products interacted with in Window B. Include all customers who churned in Q1 2023, even if they had no interactions in one or both windows (in which case the count should be 0).
Order the results by `churn_date` in ascending order, then by `customer_id` in ascending order.
