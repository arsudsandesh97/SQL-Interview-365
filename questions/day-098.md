# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk High-Value Customers

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "TrendVault," an e-commerce retailer specializing in fashion and accessories. The marketing team is concerned about potential churn among their most valuable customers. They suspect that some historically high-spending customers have significantly reduced their purchasing activity recently, indicating they might be "at-risk" and require targeted re-engagement campaigns.

Your task is to identify these at-risk customers by comparing their recent spending habits to their historical patterns.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify "At-Risk High-Value Customers" based on the following criteria:

1.  **High-Value Customer:** A customer whose total lifetime spend (sum of all `total_amount` from their orders) is greater than or equal to `1500.00`.
2.  **Recent Spending Period:** The 3 full calendar months immediately preceding the month of `CURRENT_DATE`. (For `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`, this period is July, August, and September 2023).
3.  **Historical Spending Period:** The 12 full calendar months immediately preceding the Recent Spending Period. (For `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`, this period is July 2022 to June 2023).
4.  **At-Risk Condition:** A high-value customer is considered "At-Risk" if their **average monthly spend** in the Recent Spending Period is less than 50% of their **average monthly spend** in the Historical Spending Period. If the historical average monthly spend is zero, and recent average monthly spend is also zero, they are considered "Healthy". If historical average is positive and recent is zero, they are "At-Risk".

Your query should return the `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `lifetime_spend`, `recent_3_month_avg_spend`, `historical_12_month_avg_spend`, and a `status` column indicating 'At-Risk' or 'Healthy'.
Round all average spend values to 2 decimal places.
