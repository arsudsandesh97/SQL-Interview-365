# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Plan Change Revenue Impact

## Business Scenario
You are a Senior Data Analyst at a fast-growing SaaS company. Your team is responsible for understanding customer subscription behavior and its financial implications. Stakeholders want to gain insight into the monthly revenue impact caused by users upgrading or downgrading their subscription plans. They need a consolidated report showing the total revenue gained from upgrades, total revenue lost from downgrades, and the net revenue impact for each month where such changes occurred.

An **upgrade** is defined as a user transitioning from a lower-priced plan to a higher-priced plan.
A **downgrade** is defined as a user transitioning from a higher-priced plan to a lower-priced plan.
For any given plan change, only consider the immediate previous plan of that user for price comparison. Initial subscriptions (a user's very first plan entry) should not be counted as an upgrade or downgrade.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-07-20'
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE plans (
    plan_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    plan_name VARCHAR(50),
    monthly_price DECIMAL(10, 2)
);
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to calculate the total monthly revenue gained from upgrades and total monthly revenue lost from downgrades. The report should summarize these metrics for each month where plan changes occurred. Exclude initial subscriptions from upgrade/downgrade calculations.

Your output should include the following columns:
- `analysis_month`: The first day of the month (e.g., '2023-01-01').
- `total_upgrade_revenue_gain`: Total revenue increase from all upgrades in that month.
- `total_downgrade_revenue_loss`: Total revenue decrease from all downgrades in that month.
- `net_revenue_impact`: `total_upgrade_revenue_gain` - `total_downgrade_revenue_loss`.

Order the results by `analysis_month`.
