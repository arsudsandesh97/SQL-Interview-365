# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Analyzing E-commerce Order Delays and Inventory Impact

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst for a growing E-commerce company. The Operations team is concerned about customer satisfaction due to frequent order delays. They suspect that certain product categories, especially those experiencing low stock frequently, contribute disproportionately to these delays. Your task is to analyze order fulfillment data to identify orders with significant delays and investigate the relationship between these delays and the initial stock levels of the items within those orders. Specifically, they want to understand which product categories are most affected by delays and, for those delayed orders, what percentage of them contained items that were 'low stock' at the time of order placement within that specific category.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

```sql

    order_item_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    order_id INT,
    product_id INT,
```

# Task
Identify all orders that experienced a "significant delay", defined as orders where the `actual_delivery_date` was more than 3 days after the `estimated_delivery_date`. For each distinct `category` of products, calculate the following metrics:

1.  **`total_delayed_orders`**: The total number of significantly delayed orders that contained at least one product from that category.
2.  **`avg_delay_days`**: The average delay duration (in days, rounded to 1 decimal place) for orders identified in point 1.
3.  **`percentage_delayed_with_low_stock`**: The percentage of significantly delayed orders (from point 1) where *at least one item belonging to that specific category* within the order was 'low stock' at the time of order placement. An item is considered 'low stock' if its `initial_stock_level_at_order_time` was less than its `quantity` in the order.

The results should be ordered by `total_delayed_orders` in descending order.

---

| category    | total_delayed_orders | avg_delay_days | percentage_delayed_with_low_stock |
| :---------- | :------------------- | :------------- | :-------------------------------- |
| Electronics | 3                    | 4.7            | 66.7                              |
| Apparel     | 3                    | 5.0            | 66.7                              |
