# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Customers with Slowing Purchase Frequency (Proactive Retention)

## Business Scenario
An e-commerce company is focusing on proactive customer retention. They want to identify customers who might be disengaging *before* they completely churn. They believe a significant slowdown in a customer's purchasing frequency is an early warning sign. As a Data Analyst, you need to develop a SQL query to pinpoint these "at-risk" customers.

**Definition of an "At-Risk for Slowing Frequency" customer:**
1.  They have made at least 3 purchases overall.
2.  Their last purchase occurred within the last 90 days from `CURRENT_DATE`. This ensures we focus on recently active customers who might be slowing down, rather than already churned ones.
3.  The average days between their *last two consecutive purchases* is at least `1.5 times` greater than their *overall average days between all consecutive purchases*.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Database Setup

## Create Customers Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    first_name VARCHAR(50),
    last_name VARCHAR(50),
    email VARCHAR(100)
);
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify "Proactive Retention Candidates" based on the criteria defined in the business scenario.
The output should list `customer_id`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`, their `last_purchase_date`, their `overall_avg_days_between_purchases` (rounded to 2 decimal places), and the `avg_days_between_last_two_purchases` (rounded to 2 decimal places). Order the results by `customer_id`.
