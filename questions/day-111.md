# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Subscription Downgrade Duration Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a growing SaaS company that offers various subscription plans: Free, Basic, Standard, and Premium. The customer success and marketing teams are focusing on reducing churn and improving customer retention. They've observed that users who downgrade their plan might be at a higher risk of churning. To better understand this behavior, they want to analyze how long users typically stay on a *downgraded* plan before making another subscription change (e.g., upgrading again, downgrading further, or cancelling) or if they are still on that downgraded plan. Your task is to provide an analysis of these downgrade events, including the duration a user spent on each downgraded plan.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify all instances where a user downgraded their subscription plan. For each downgrade event, calculate the duration (in days) the user remained on the *downgraded* plan.

The output should include:
1. `user_id`
2. `downgrade_effective_date` (the `effective_date` of the downgraded plan)
3. `previous_plan` (the plan immediately before the downgrade)
4. `downgraded_to_plan` (the plan after the downgrade)
5. `duration_on_downgraded_plan_days` (the number of days the user stayed on the `downgraded_to_plan`).
   - This duration should be calculated from the `downgrade_effective_date` up to the `effective_date` of their *next* subscription change.
   - If there is no subsequent subscription change after the downgrade (i.e., it's their current plan), calculate the duration from `downgrade_effective_date` up to `CURRENT_DATE`.

Order the results by `user_id` and then `downgrade_effective_date`.
