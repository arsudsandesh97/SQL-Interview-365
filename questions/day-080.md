# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Early Churners After Trial Conversion

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "Subscribr", a growing e-commerce platform that recently launched a premium subscription service offering exclusive discounts and faster shipping. The marketing and product teams are keenly interested in understanding customer retention, especially for those who convert from a free trial to a paid subscription. They've noticed a significant number of customers cancelling their paid subscription shortly after converting from a trial. Your task is to identify these "early churners" among trial converters to help the teams understand the problem better and formulate retention strategies. The definition of "early churn" for this analysis is cancelling within 60 days of the *start date of their first paid subscription*.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
```

```sql

    subscription_id INT PRIMARY KEY,

    end_date DATE, -- NULL if active
    FOREIGN KEY (customer_id) REFERENCES Customers(customer_id)
```

# Task
Your task is to identify all customers who meet the following criteria:
1.  They successfully completed a 'Trial' subscription.
2.  They subsequently converted to their *first paid* subscription ('Monthly' or 'Annual').
3.  They then *churned* (their `end_date` is not NULL) within 60 days of the `start_date` of that first paid subscription.

For each identified customer, return their `customer_id`, `customer_name`, the `start_date` of their first paid subscription, the `end_date` of that subscription, and the `churn_days` (the number of days from the `start_date` to the `end_date` of their first paid subscription).

Order the results by `customer_id`.
