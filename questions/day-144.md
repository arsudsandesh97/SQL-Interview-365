# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Monthly Net MRR Change Analysis by Event Type

## Business Scenario
A rapidly growing SaaS company, 'CloudFlow CRM', wants to gain deeper insights into its monthly recurring revenue (MRR) dynamics. As a Data Analyst, you've been tasked with breaking down the changes in MRR each month. Specifically, the Head of Finance needs to understand how much MRR is gained from new subscriptions, how much is gained/lost from existing customers upgrading or downgrading their plans, and how much is lost due to cancellations. This analysis will help them forecast revenue more accurately and identify key growth drivers and churn indicators.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-03-01'
```

# Task
Calculate the monthly net MRR change for each month. The net change should be broken down into the following components:
1.  **New_MRR**: The sum of `monthly_price` for new subscriptions (i.e., the *first ever* `SUBSCRIBE` event for a given `customer_id`) that occurred in that month.
2.  **Upgrade_MRR_Change**: The sum of positive differences in `monthly_price` (new plan price - old plan price) for `UPGRADE` events that occurred in that month.
3.  **Downgrade_MRR_Change**: The sum of negative differences in `monthly_price` (new plan price - old plan price) for `DOWNGRADE` events that occurred in that month.
4.  **Cancellation_MRR_Loss**: The sum of `monthly_price` (as a negative value) for plans associated with `CANCEL` events that occurred in that month.

Your output should include `analysis_month`, `New_MRR`, `Upgrade_MRR_Change`, `Downgrade_MRR_Change`, and `Cancellation_MRR_Loss`. Only include months where there was at least one event contributing to MRR change.
