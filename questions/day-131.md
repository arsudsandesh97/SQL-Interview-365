# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Customers by Declining Purchase Frequency

## Business Scenario
As a Data Analyst at "E-Shop Innovations," an e-commerce platform, your marketing team is focused on customer retention. They suspect that customers whose purchase frequency has significantly dropped are 'at-risk' of churning and want to target them with re-engagement campaigns.

Your task is to identify these 'at-risk' customers. A customer is considered 'at-risk' if their total number of orders in the last 60 days is less than 50% of their average order count for a typical 60-day period, calculated from their entire purchasing history *prior to the last 60 days*. Only consider customers who have made at least one purchase in their historical period (i.e., prior to the last 60 days).

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Database Setup

## Create Customers Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_name VARCHAR(100),
    registration_date DATE
);
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify 'at-risk' customers based on the criteria:
1.  Their total number of orders in the last 60 days (relative to `CURRENT_DATE`) is less than 50% of their average order count for a typical 60-day period.
2.  The "average order count for a typical 60-day period" should be calculated from their entire purchase history *prior to the last 60 days*.
3.  Only include customers who have made at least one purchase in their historical period (i.e., *before* the last 60 days). Customers whose first order is within the last 60 days or who have no orders prior to the last 60 days should be excluded.

The output should include `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `historical_avg_60day_orders` (the calculated average 60-day rate), and `recent_60day_orders` (their actual order count in the last 60 days). Round `historical_avg_60day_orders` to 2 decimal places.

---
