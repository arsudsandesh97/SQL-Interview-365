# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identify At-Risk SaaS Customers by Feature Usage Decline

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "CloudPilot Solutions," a SaaS company providing project management and collaboration tools. The customer success team is concerned about proactive churn prevention. They believe that a significant decline in a customer's engagement with a *critical feature* is an early indicator of dissatisfaction and potential churn. Your task is to develop a SQL query that identifies customers who have shown a sharp drop in their weekly usage of the "Advanced Reporting" feature compared to their recent average. This will help the customer success managers intervene before it's too late.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-20'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create Customers Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_name VARCHAR(100),
    subscription_start_date DATE,
    plan_type VARCHAR(50)
);
```


# Task
Identify all customers who meet the following criteria:
1.  They have actively used the 'Advanced Reporting' feature.
2.  Their weekly usage count for 'Advanced Reporting' in the *most recently completed full week* is less than 50% of their average weekly usage count for the *preceding four full weeks*.
3.  The *most recently completed full week* is the full calendar week immediately prior to `CURRENT_DATE`. (E.g., if `CURRENT_DATE` is Monday, November 20, 2023, the most recently completed full week starts Monday, November 13, 2023, and ends Sunday, November 19, 2023).

Return `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `current_week_usage_count`, and `four_week_avg_usage`. Only include customers who have at least one full week of data in the 4-week preceding period to calculate a meaningful average.

---

| customer_id | customer_name | current_week_usage_count | four_week_avg_usage |
