# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Customers with Declining Engagement

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "TrendVista," an e-commerce fashion retailer. The marketing team is planning a re-engagement campaign and wants to identify customers who have shown a significant drop in their purchasing activity recently, indicating they might be "losing interest." They want to target customers who have historically been active but whose recent engagement (both frequency and average order value) has noticeably declined compared to their prior period.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE Orders (

    customer_id INT NOT NULL,
    order_date DATE NOT NULL,
```

# Task
Identify `customer_id`s who meet **all** of the following criteria based on `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`:

1.  The customer has made at least one purchase in the last 6 months (i.e., `order_date` between `'2023-04-27'` and `'2023-10-26'`, inclusive).
2.  Their **average monthly order count** in the last 3 months (L3M: `'2023-07-27'` to `'2023-10-26'`) is strictly less than their average monthly order count in the 3 months prior to that (P3M: `'2023-04-27'` to `'2023-07-26'`).
3.  Their **average order value (AOV)** in the last 3 months (L3M) is strictly less than their AOV in the 3 months prior (P3M).

Return the `customer_id`, their average monthly order count for L3M (`avg_orders_l3m`), their AOV for L3M (`avg_aov_l3m`), their average monthly order count for P3M (`avg_orders_p3m`), and their AOV for P3M (`avg_aov_p3m`).
Only include customers who have at least one order in both the L3M and P3M periods for comparison.
