# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Discount Impact on Customer Reorder Velocity

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "BeanBox Subscriptions," an e-commerce company specializing in monthly coffee subscription boxes. The marketing team recently ran a promotion offering a discount on a customer's first-ever subscription box. They want to understand if this "first-order discount" strategy leads to higher customer retention and engagement in the long run, specifically focusing on how quickly customers place subsequent orders compared to those who did not receive such a discount on their initial purchase.

Your task is to analyze the reorder velocity by comparing the average days between subsequent orders for two distinct customer groups: those whose very first order included the discount, and those whose first order did not.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create Orders Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Orders (
    order_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_id VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    order_date DATE NOT NULL,
    order_total_usd DECIMAL(10, 2) NOT NULL,
    discount_applied_flag BOOLEAN NOT NULL
);
```

# Task
Calculate the average days between the 1st and 2nd orders, and the average days between the 2nd and 3rd orders, for two distinct groups of customers:
1.  **`Discounted First Purchase`**: Customers whose very first order had `discount_applied_flag = TRUE`.
2.  **`Non-Discounted First Purchase`**: Customers whose very first order had `discount_applied_flag = FALSE`.

If a group or a specific customer within a group does not have enough orders to calculate a particular average (e.g., no 2nd order to calculate 1st to 2nd average), that value should be `NULL` or 0 for the average.

The final output should display two rows, one for each customer group, with the calculated average days.

---
