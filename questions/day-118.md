# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk SaaS Customers by Feature Usage Decline

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "CloudInsight Analytics," a SaaS company offering various data analytics tools. Your primary product is a dashboarding service, and the 'Analytics Dashboard Views' feature is considered critical for customer retention. The Customer Success team wants to proactively identify customers who are on active annual plans, whose subscriptions are due for renewal soon (within the next 90 days), and who show signs of disengagement based on a significant drop in their usage of the 'Analytics Dashboard Views' feature, or a very old last usage date for this feature. This allows the team to intervene before churn.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `email`, `plan_name`, `renewal_date`, `last_feature_usage_date`, `recent_30_day_avg_usage`, and `prev_6_month_avg_usage` for active customers who meet **ALL** the following criteria:
1.  They are currently on an 'Annual' plan.
2.  Their `renewal_date` is within the next 90 days from `CURRENT_DATE`.
3.  Their `status` is 'active'.
4.  They show signs of disengagement, defined as **EITHER**:
    *   (`prev_6_month_avg_usage` > 0 AND `recent_30_day_avg_usage` < 0.5 * `prev_6_month_avg_usage`)
        *   `recent_30_day_avg_usage` is the average daily `usage_count` for 'Analytics Dashboard Views' in the last 30 days ending on `CURRENT_DATE`.
        *   `prev_6_month_avg_usage` is the average daily `usage_count` for 'Analytics Dashboard Views' in the 6-month period immediately preceding the last 30 days (i.e., from 7 months ago up to 31 days ago, ending on `CURRENT_DATE - 30 days`).
    *   OR their `last_feature_usage_date` for 'Analytics Dashboard Views' is more than 90 days ago from `CURRENT_DATE`.

If a customer has no usage data for a particular period (e.g., last 30 days or previous 6 months), assume 0 usage for that period for the average calculation. If there is no usage data at all for the 'Analytics Dashboard Views' feature for a customer, `last_feature_usage_date` should be `NULL`.
