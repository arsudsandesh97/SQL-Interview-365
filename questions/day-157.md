# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Customers Based on Purchase Behavior Decline

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company's analytics team needs to proactively identify customers who are showing early signs of disengagement *before* they fully churn. These "at-risk" customers are valuable targets for re-engagement campaigns. The company defines an at-risk customer as someone whose purchasing behavior (both frequency and total spend) has significantly declined in the most recent full calendar month compared to their own average behavior over the preceding six full calendar months.

You, as a Data Analyst, are tasked with providing a list of these customers.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2024-06-15'
```
This means:
*   The 'most recent full calendar month' is May 2024.
*   The 'preceding six full calendar months' are November 2023, December 2023, January 2024, February 2024, March 2024, and April 2024.

Conditions for identifying an "at-risk" customer:
1.  The customer must have made purchases in at least 6 out of the 7 months considered (i.e., the most recent full calendar month + the 6 preceding full calendar months). This ensures sufficient historical data for a meaningful comparison.
2.  Their *average monthly purchase frequency* (number of orders) in the most recent full calendar month (May 2024) must be at least 25% lower than their *average monthly purchase frequency* over the preceding six full calendar months (Nov 2023 - Apr 2024).
3.  Their *average monthly total spend* in the most recent full calendar month (May 2024) must be at least 25% lower than their *average monthly total spend* over the preceding six full calendar months (Nov 2023 - Apr 2024).
4.  Both conditions (2 and 3) must be met for a customer to be classified as "at-risk".
