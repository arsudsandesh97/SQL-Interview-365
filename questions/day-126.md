# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying High-Value At-Risk E-commerce Customers

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a growing e-commerce company. The marketing team wants to launch a re-engagement campaign targeting customers who were previously valuable but haven't made a purchase recently. They need your help to identify these "High-Value At-Risk" customers. The goal is to proactively reach out to them before they churn completely.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create Customers Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    first_name VARCHAR(50),
    last_name VARCHAR(50),
    email VARCHAR(100) UNIQUE,
    registration_date DATE
);
```

# Task
Identify all "High-Value At-Risk" customers. A customer is defined as "High-Value At-Risk" if they meet the following criteria:

1.  **Made at least one purchase:** The customer has at least one order recorded.
2.  **Inactive recently:** Their last purchase date is more than 60 days prior to `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'`.
3.  **High lifetime value:** Their total lifetime spend (sum of `total_amount` from all their orders) is greater than the average total lifetime spend of *all customers who have made at least one purchase*.

Return the `customer_id`, `email`, `total_lifetime_spend`, and `days_since_last_purchase`. Order the results by `days_since_last_purchase` in descending order.
