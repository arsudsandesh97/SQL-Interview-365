# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: E-commerce Product Inventory Forecast and Stock-Out Risk Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Senior Data Analyst at "StockWise," a growing e-commerce company specializing in electronics. Your operations team is struggling to proactively manage inventory, often leading to either overstocking or critical stock-outs for popular products. They need a system to predict future inventory levels and quickly identify potential stock-out risks over a specific future period, considering both daily sales forecasts and scheduled inbound shipments. This insight is crucial for making timely reordering decisions and optimizing logistics.

Your task is to develop a SQL query that provides a 30-day projected inventory level for key products, highlights the minimum inventory they will reach, and flags if they are expected to hit zero before a scheduled shipment arrives within that period.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Generate a report that projects the inventory level for each product over the next 30 days starting from `CURRENT_DATE`. For each product, the report should include:

1.  `product_name`
2.  `current_stock` (as of `CURRENT_DATE`)
3.  `min_projected_inventory` (the lowest inventory level projected over the 30-day period, considering sales and shipments)
4.  `first_shipment_date` (the earliest scheduled shipment date within the 30-day period for that product, or NULL if none)
5.  `stock_out_before_shipment_flag` (a boolean/integer flag: `1` if the product's projected inventory drops to 0 or below *before* its `first_shipment_date` within the 30-day window, `0` otherwise. If there are no shipments, consider the entire 30-day period for potential stock-out.)

The projection should simulate daily inventory changes:
*   Start with `current_stock` on `CURRENT_DATE`.
*   Each day, subtract `forecasted_quantity` from `DailySalesForecast`.
*   If a `shipment_date` from `InboundShipments` matches the current day, add `quantity_expected` to the inventory *after* daily sales are subtracted for that day.

Your solution should handle cases where:
*   There are no sales forecasts for certain days (assume 0 sales).
*   There are no inbound shipments for a product within the period.
*   The `first_shipment_date` is outside the 30-day window, or there are no shipments at all.
