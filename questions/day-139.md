# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: E-commerce Customer Journey: First Category Purchase and Related Cross-Sell Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a growing E-commerce company. The marketing and product teams are keenly interested in understanding the early purchasing behavior of new customers, particularly those whose *very first interaction* with the platform involved a significant purchase. They want to identify customers whose *initial order* included an item from the 'Electronics' category and then subsequently purchased an 'Accessory' item within 30 days of that first 'Electronics' order. This analysis will help evaluate the effectiveness of initial product recommendations or cross-selling strategies related to electronic items.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify the `customer_id`, their `customer_name`, the `first_electronics_order_date`, and the `subsequent_accessory_order_date` for all customers who meet the following criteria:

1.  Their *very first order* (chronologically, across all categories) on the platform contained at least one item from the 'Electronics' category.
2.  They subsequently made another order that contained at least one item from the 'Accessory' category.
3.  This subsequent 'Accessory' purchase occurred within 30 days of their *first order date* (which contained Electronics).
4.  If a customer made multiple qualifying 'Accessory' purchases within the 30-day window, report the *earliest* one.
