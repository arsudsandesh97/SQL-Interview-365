# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying High-Potential Quick Upgraders in SaaS

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a growing SaaS company focused on B2B collaboration tools. The marketing and sales teams want to identify a specific segment of users: "High-Potential Quick Upgraders." These are users who started with a low-tier (Free Trial or Basic) plan, quickly recognized the value, and upgraded to a high-tier (Premium or Enterprise) plan, and have maintained that high-tier status consistently. Identifying these users helps the sales team understand what resonates with them, potentially inform product development, and refine lead scoring models.

Your task is to identify these "High-Potential Quick Upgraders" based on their subscription history.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-07-01'
```

## Create subscription_plans Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE subscription_plans (
    plan_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    plan_name VARCHAR(100),
    monthly_price DECIMAL(10, 2),
    plan_tier VARCHAR(50) -- e.g., 'Free Trial', 'Basic', 'Premium', 'Enterprise'
);
```

## Create subscriptions Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE subscriptions (
    subscription_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    user_id INT REFERENCES users(user_id),
    plan_id INT REFERENCES subscription_plans(plan_id),
    start_date DATE,
    end_date DATE NULL -- NULL indicates the subscription is currently active
);
```

# Task
Identify all "High-Potential Quick Upgraders" by listing their `user_id`, their `first_subscription_date`, the `first_high_tier_upgrade_date`, and the `days_to_upgrade` (number of days from their first subscription to their first high-tier upgrade).

A user qualifies as a "High-Potential Quick Upgrader" if they meet **ALL** of the following criteria:

1.  **Initial Plan Type:** Their very first subscription (based on `start_date`) was a 'Free Trial' or 'Basic' plan.
2.  **Quick Upgrade:** They upgraded to a 'Premium' or 'Enterprise' plan within **30 days** of their `first_subscription_date`.
3.  **Current High-Tier:** As of `CURRENT_DATE`, they have an active 'Premium' or 'Enterprise' plan. (An active plan has `end_date IS NULL` and `start_date <= CURRENT_DATE`).
4.  **Sustained High-Tier:** There is no record of them having an active 'Basic' or 'Free Trial' plan at any point within the last 90 days leading up to `CURRENT_DATE`. This means for any subscription `S` with `S.start_date <= CURRENT_DATE` and `(S.end_date IS NULL OR S.end_date >= CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '90 days')`, its `plan_tier` must NOT be 'Basic' or 'Free Trial'. The 90-day window is `[CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '90 days', CURRENT_DATE]`.
