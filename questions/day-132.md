# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Emerging High-Return Customers

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company is focused on improving customer retention and understanding purchasing behavior shifts. They've observed that some customers, after an initial period of satisfactory purchasing, start exhibiting a significantly higher return rate, which could signal dissatisfaction or a change in buying habits. The Marketing and Customer Success teams want to proactively identify these customers to implement targeted interventions, such as personalized recommendations or support, before they potentially churn.

Your task is to identify customers who, after their initial 180 days of activity, show a concerning increase in their return rate during their subsequent 180 days.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify customers who meet the following criteria:
1.  They have activity (orders) spanning at least 360 days from their first order date (i.e., their 360th day from the first order must be on or before `CURRENT_DATE`).
2.  Their 'return quantity rate' (total returned quantity / total purchased quantity) in their *second* 180-day period (days 181-360 from their first order) is at least `0.10` (10 percentage points) higher than their return quantity rate in their *first* 180-day period (days 0-180 from their first order).
3.  Their 'return quantity rate' in the *second* 180-day period is also greater than `0.20` (20%).

The query should return `CustomerID`, `FirstOrderDate`, `ReturnRate_Initial180Days`, and `ReturnRate_Subsequent180Days`.
Return rates should be presented as decimals, rounded to 4 decimal places. If `TotalPurchasedQuantity` is 0 for a period, treat the `ReturnRate` as 0.

---
