# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Customer Primary Category Shift Analysis

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "Trendify," an e-commerce platform. The marketing team is interested in identifying customers whose primary product category preference has recently shifted. Understanding these shifts can help in targeted re-engagement campaigns, identifying emerging trends, or evaluating the success of cross-selling initiatives. Your task is to find customers who have changed their most purchased product category when comparing their recent buying habits to their previous buying habits.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify `customer_id`s who have shifted their primary product category preference. A customer's primary product category for a given period is defined as the category from which they purchased the highest total `quantity` of items. In case of a tie in total quantity across multiple categories, any one of the tied categories is acceptable as the primary.

We need to compare two distinct 3-month periods:
1.  **Recent 3-month period:** From `CURRENT_DATE - 3 months + 1 day` to `CURRENT_DATE` (inclusive). For `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`, this is `2023-07-27` to `2023-10-26`.
2.  **Previous 3-month period:** From `CURRENT_DATE - 6 months + 1 day` to `CURRENT_DATE - 3 months` (inclusive). For `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`, this is `2023-04-27` to `2023-07-26`.

Only include customers who have made purchases in *both* periods and whose primary category in the recent period is *different* from their primary category in the previous period.
