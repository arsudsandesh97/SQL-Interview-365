# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS MRR Movement Analysis

## Business Scenario
A rapidly growing SaaS company, "CloudSolutions Inc.", tracks its Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) diligently to understand subscriber behavior and revenue growth drivers. They maintain a snapshot of each customer's total MRR at the end of every month. The Head of Finance needs a report that quantifies the MRR movement (upgrades, downgrades, churn) for a specific month, focusing only on customers who were active at the beginning of that month. This report will help them identify trends in customer expansion and contraction.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-06-15'
```

---

# Database Setup

## Create customer_mrr_snapshots Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE customer_mrr_snapshots (
    snapshot_date DATE NOT NULL,
    customer_id INT NOT NULL,
    mrr_value DECIMAL(10, 2) NOT NULL,
    PRIMARY KEY (snapshot_date, customer_id)
);
```

## Insert customer_mrr_snapshots Data
```sql
INSERT INTO customer_mrr_snapshots (snapshot_date, customer_id, mrr_value) VALUES
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to analyze the MRR movement for the month of **May 2023**.
Your query should identify customers who were active at the end of April 2023 (i.e., `mrr_value > 0` on `2023-04-30`) and categorize their MRR change by:
1.  **Upgrade**: Customer's MRR increased between `2023-04-30` and `2023-05-31`.
2.  **Downgrade**: Customer's MRR decreased between `2023-04-30` and `2023-05-31`, but is still `> 0` on `2023-05-31`.
3.  **Churn**: Customer's MRR decreased between `2023-04-30` and `2023-05-31`, and is `0` on `2023-05-31`.

For each category, report the `MRR_Movement_Type`, the `Number_Of_Customers` in that category, and the `Total_MRR_Change` (sum of `(mrr_value_on_2023_05_31 - mrr_value_on_2023_04_30)`) for those customers. Exclude customers whose MRR did not change.
