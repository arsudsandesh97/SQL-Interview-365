# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Coffee Category Repurchase Analysis Post-Promotion

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at an e-commerce company specializing in gourmet food and beverages. The marketing team recently ran a significant promotional campaign throughout September 2023 specifically targeting 'Coffee' category products. They want to understand the effectiveness of this campaign in driving long-term customer engagement within the coffee category.

Specifically, they are interested in comparing the 60-day repurchase rate for coffee products between two distinct customer segments who made at least one coffee purchase during the promotion month (September 2023):
1.  **New Coffee Buyers:** Customers whose *first ever* purchase in the 'Coffee' category occurred in September 2023.
2.  **Existing Coffee Buyers:** Customers who had previously purchased a 'Coffee' category product *before* September 2023, and also made at least one 'Coffee' purchase in September 2023.

The marketing team needs these insights to evaluate their promotional strategy and decide on future campaign targeting.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-12-01'
```

# Task
Calculate the 60-day coffee category repurchase rate for two customer segments who bought *any* coffee product during the promotion month (September 2023):
1.  **New Coffee Buyers:** Customers whose *first ever* purchase in the 'Coffee' category occurred in September 2023.
2.  **Existing Coffee Buyers:** Customers who had previously purchased a 'Coffee' category product *before* September 2023, and also made at least one 'Coffee' purchase in September 2023.

The repurchase rate should be calculated as: `(Number of unique customers making a subsequent coffee purchase within 60 days of their *last* coffee purchase in September 2023) / (Total unique customers in that segment)`.
The results should be presented as `customer_segment` and `repurchase_rate` (rounded to 2 decimal places).

---

| customer_segment   | repurchase_rate |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| New Coffee Buyers  | 0.50            |
| Existing Coffee Buyers | 0.50            |

# Skills Tested
*   **CTE Usage:** Structuring complex queries with Common Table Expressions for readability and modularity.
*   **Window Functions:** Identifying first/last purchases within a category per customer (`MIN/MAX OVER (PARTITION BY ...)`).
*   **Date Functions:** Filtering by month/year, calculating date differences (`DATE_TRUNC`, `DATE_DIFF` or equivalent, `INTERVAL`).
*   **Conditional Logic & Aggregation:** Using `CASE` statements for segmentation and calculating conditional counts.
*   **Joins:** Combining data from multiple tables (`Orders`, `Order_Items`, `Products`).
*   **Data Segmentation:** Differentiating customers based on historical behavior and promotion period purchases.
*   **Repurchase Rate Calculation:** Implementing the specific business logic for repurchase.
