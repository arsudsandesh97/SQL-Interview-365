# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identify At-Risk Renewing Customers by Usage Decline

## Business Scenario
A leading SaaS company offers subscription-based software. Their customer success team is trying to proactively identify "at-risk" customers who *did* renew their subscription but showed a significant drop-off in product usage leading up to their renewal. This early warning signal helps them engage with these customers to understand potential dissatisfaction and prevent future churn, even if they've renewed for another term.

Your task is to identify such customers based on their event activity.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2024-05-01'
```

---

    subscription_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    user_id INT NOT NULL,

# Task
Find all `user_id`s who renewed their subscription and whose average daily event count in the 30 days immediately preceding their renewal date (Period 1) was at least 25% lower than their average daily event count in the 30-day period before that (Period 2: 31-60 days before renewal date).

Only consider renewals where the `start_date` of the new subscription immediately follows the `end_date` of the previous subscription (i.e., `new_sub.start_date = old_sub.end_date + 1 day`). The `renewal_date` for analysis should be the `end_date` of the *previous* subscription.
Exclude users if their Period 2 average daily event count was zero (as a 25% drop from zero is not meaningful).
The average daily event count should be calculated by dividing the total events in the period by 30.0 (for 30 days).
