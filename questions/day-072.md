# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Monthly Subscription MRR Movement Analysis

## Business Scenario
As a Data Analyst at 'CloudFlow Inc.', a growing SaaS company, you are tasked with analyzing the monthly dynamics of subscriptions and their impact on Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR). The Product and Finance teams require a detailed breakdown of how MRR changes from month to month, specifically identifying the MRR gained from new subscribers, lost due to churn, and shifted by upgrades/downgrades and reactivations within a given month. This analysis is critical for understanding business growth drivers and informing strategic decisions.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-15'
```
The analysis needs to be performed for the full month of `October 2023`.

# Database Setup

```sql
CREATE TABLE users (
```


# Task
For the month of `October 2023` (based on `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-15'`), calculate the net MRR change, categorized into the following types:

1.  **New Subscriptions MRR**: Total `monthly_mrr_value` from users whose *first ever* subscription period (`start_date`) falls within October 2023.
2.  **Churn MRR**: Total `monthly_mrr_value` from plans that ended (`end_date` falls within October 2023) for users who *do not* have any active subscription period at the end of October 2023. This is MRR *lost*.
3.  **Upgrade MRR Change**: The *net increase* in `monthly_mrr_value` for users who transitioned from a lower-priced plan to a higher-priced plan within October 2023. Calculate `(new_plan_mrr - old_plan_mrr)` for each upgrade.
4.  **Downgrade MRR Change**: The *net decrease* in `monthly_mrr_value` for users who transitioned from a higher-priced plan to a lower-priced plan within October 2023. Calculate `(new_plan_mrr - old_plan_mrr)` for each downgrade (this will be a negative value).
5.  **Reactivation MRR**: Total `monthly_mrr_value` from users who had a previous subscription period that ended *before* October 2023, and then started a *new* subscription period within October 2023.

The final output should show each category and its corresponding total MRR change.
