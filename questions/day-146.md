# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Analyzing Customer Win-Back Performance

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at 'SubscribrNow', an e-commerce company offering various subscription box services. The marketing and customer retention teams are keen on understanding the effectiveness of their 'win-back' strategies. A customer is considered 'won back' if they previously had an active subscription that ended (either by cancellation or non-renewal) and subsequently started a *new* subscription within a specific `win_back_period_days` (e.g., 90 days) from the `end_date` of their *previous* subscription. Your task is to identify these 'won-back' instances and calculate the duration of their inactive period to help assess campaign success and optimize future retention efforts.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify all instances where a customer was 'won back'. A customer is won back if they start a new subscription within 90 days (the `win_back_period_days`) after their previous subscription ended.

Your query should return the following columns for each 'won-back' instance:
1.  `customer_id`
2.  `new_subscription_start_date` (the `start_date` of the subscription that signifies the win-back)
3.  `previous_subscription_end_date` (the `end_date` of the subscription *before* the new one)
4.  `inactive_period_days` (the number of days between the `previous_subscription_end_date` and `new_subscription_start_date`)
