# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Customer Loyalty and Churn Segmentation

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company's marketing team wants to refine their customer retention strategies. They need to categorize their customer base to identify loyal customers, those at risk of churning, and those who have likely churned. Specifically, they are interested in customers who have shown initial engagement by making at least two purchases but need a clear segmentation based on their recent activity to tailor targeted campaigns. As a Data Analyst, you've been asked to provide this segmentation.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query that identifies and categorizes customers based on their purchasing behavior.
For each customer who has made *at least two purchases*, calculate:
1.  `customer_id`
2.  `customer_name`
3.  `total_purchases` (the total count of orders made by the customer)
4.  `days_since_last_purchase` (the number of days between `CURRENT_DATE` and their `last_order_date`)
5.  `customer_segment` based on the following rules:
    *   'Active': If `days_since_last_purchase` <= 90
    *   'Lapsing': If `days_since_last_purchase` > 90 AND `days_since_last_purchase` <= 180
    *   'Churned': If `days_since_last_purchase` > 180

Order the results by `customer_id` ascending.
