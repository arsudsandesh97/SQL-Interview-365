# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Customer Engagement Drop-offs

## Business Scenario
An e-commerce company is trying to proactively identify customers who are showing signs of disengagement. They believe that a significant drop in a customer's varied activity (not just purchases, but also browsing, adding to cart, wishlisting) is a strong indicator of potential churn. As a Data Analyst, you need to develop a report that flags customers whose average daily engagement diversity has significantly decreased in the last 30 days compared to their engagement diversity in the 31-90 days prior.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE customers (
    customer_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
```

# Task
Identify `customer_id` and `customer_name` of customers who are at "high engagement drop-off risk". A customer is considered to be at high engagement drop-off risk if:

1.  Their average daily distinct activity types in the **recent 30-day window** (from `CURRENT_DATE - 30 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 1 day`) is less than 50% of their average daily distinct activity types in the **past 60-day window** (from `CURRENT_DATE - 90 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 31 days`).
2.  They had at least an average of **0.1 distinct activity types per day** in the **past 60-day window**. This is to filter out customers who were never highly engaged to begin with.

The "average daily distinct activity types" for a window is calculated as the sum of `COUNT(DISTINCT activity_type)` for each day (a day with no activity counts as 0 distinct types) within the window, divided by the total number of days in that window.

The output should include `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `avg_distinct_activities_recent` (rounded to 2 decimal places), `avg_distinct_activities_past` (rounded to 2 decimal places), and `percentage_drop` (calculated as `(1 - (avg_distinct_activities_recent / avg_distinct_activities_past)) * 100`, rounded to 2 decimal places).

Consider `CURRENT_DATE` as `2023-10-26`.
- Recent 30-day window: `2023-09-26` to `2023-10-25` (inclusive)
- Past 60-day window: `2023-07-28` to `2023-09-25` (inclusive)
