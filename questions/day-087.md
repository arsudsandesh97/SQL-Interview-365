# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Churn Risk Identification Based on Usage Drop

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at a growing SaaS company that offers a platform with various features, billed monthly based on subscription plans and usage. The product team is concerned about customer churn and wants to proactively identify customers who are showing signs of disengagement. They suspect that a significant drop in recent usage compared to historical patterns could be a strong indicator of churn risk.

Your task is to identify customers whose average daily usage in the last 30 days has dropped by more than 20% compared to their average daily usage over all preceding historical activity. Only consider customers who have a history of usage to compare against.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'
```

# Database Setup

## Create Customers Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_name VARCHAR(100),
    signup_date DATE
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify customers who exhibit a significant drop in usage. A significant drop is defined as:
1.  Their average daily usage in the last 30 days (from `CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '29' DAY` to `CURRENT_DATE`, inclusive) is less than 80% of their average daily usage over all preceding historical days (any `log_date` before `CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '29' DAY`).
2.  Only consider customers who have a recorded history of usage before the last 30-day period began. This means `avg_daily_usage_HIST` must be greater than 0 and calculated over a valid period length.
3.  The results should include `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `avg_daily_usage_L30D` (average daily usage in the last 30 days), `avg_daily_usage_HIST` (average daily usage in the historical period), and `percentage_drop` (the percentage decrease from historical to recent usage).
4.  Round all average usage values and percentage drop to 2 decimal places.
