# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Promotional Return Exploiters

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company is facing challenges with promotional campaign profitability due to high return rates from certain customers. The marketing and finance teams suspect that some customers might be exploiting promotional offers by purchasing items during sales periods with the intention of returning a significant portion, thereby undermining the campaign's net revenue.

You are a Data Analyst tasked with identifying these "promotional return exploiters" within the last 12 months. Your analysis will help the company understand the financial impact of this behavior and potentially inform future customer segmentation or promotional strategies.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify "promotional return exploiters" among customers within the last 12 months from `CURRENT_DATE`. A customer is defined as a "promotional return exploiter" if they meet ALL of the following criteria:

1.  They have placed at least 3 promotional orders.
2.  Their *promotional return rate* (total refund amount for promotional orders / total sales amount for promotional orders) is at least 1.5 times greater than their *non-promotional return rate* (total refund amount for non-promotional orders / total sales amount for non-promotional orders).
    *   If a customer has no non-promotional orders (total_non_promo_sales_amount = 0), their `non_promo_return_rate` should be considered 0. In this specific case, the condition `promo_return_rate >= (1.5 * non_promo_return_rate)` simplifies to `promo_return_rate > 0` for them to be flagged as an exploiter (i.e., if they have any promotional returns with no non-promotional sales history).
    *   If total sales amount for a category (promotional or non-promotional) is 0, its return rate should be 0.
3.  All sales and returns activities must have occurred within the last 12 months (from `CURRENT_DATE`).

Calculate and return the following metrics for each identified exploiter:
- `customer_id`
- `customer_name`
- `total_promo_orders` (count of promotional orders)
- `total_promo_sales_amount` (total sales from promotional orders)
- `total_promo_refund_amount` (total refunds from promotional orders)
- `promo_return_rate` (calculated as `total_promo_refund_amount / total_promo_sales_amount`, rounded to 4 decimal places)
- `total_non_promo_sales_amount` (total sales from non-promotional orders)
- `total_non_promo_refund_amount` (total refunds from non-promotional orders)
- `non_promo_return_rate` (calculated as `total_non_promo_refund_amount / total_non_promo_sales_amount`, rounded to 4 decimal places)
- `net_promo_spend` (total_promo_sales_amount - total_promo_refund_amount)
- `net_non_promo_spend` (total_non_promo_sales_amount - total_non_promo_refund_amount)

Order the results by `customer_id`.
