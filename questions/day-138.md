# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Product Categories

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at an E-commerce company. The marketing and product teams are concerned about customer loyalty to specific product categories. They suspect that some customers, who were previously active buyers in certain categories, have stopped purchasing from those categories. Identifying these "at-risk" category segments is crucial for targeted re-engagement campaigns and product strategy adjustments.

Your task is to identify customer-category pairs where customers historically purchased from a category but have completely ceased purchasing from that same category in the most recent period.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---

# Task
Identify `customer_id` and `category` pairs that meet the following criteria:
1.  The customer made at least one purchase in that specific category during the **Historical Period**. The Historical Period is defined as the 90 days immediately preceding the last 30 days from `CURRENT_DATE`. (i.e., `CURRENT_DATE - 120 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 31 days`).
2.  The customer made **zero** purchases in the same category during the **Current Period**. The Current Period is defined as the last 30 days from `CURRENT_DATE`. (i.e., `CURRENT_DATE - 29 days` to `CURRENT_DATE`).

For these identified pairs, return the `customer_id`, `category`, the `total quantity` purchased in the Historical Period, and the `total quantity` purchased in the Current Period (which should be 0).

---

| 1003        | Electronics | 1                   | 0                |
