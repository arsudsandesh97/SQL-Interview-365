# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Customer Loyalty Segmentation by Category Diversification

## Business Scenario
An E-commerce company's marketing department wants to better understand and segment their most active customer base to optimize targeted marketing campaigns. They are particularly interested in identifying customers who are not only consistently purchasing but also diversifying their product interests into new categories versus those who remain loyal to a specific set of categories. This segmentation will help tailor personalized recommendations, cross-selling efforts, and retention strategies.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify and segment customers into two groups based on their recent activity and category diversification. The analysis should be based on `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`.

1.  **"Loyal Explorers"**: These are customers who meet *all* of the following criteria:
    *   Made at least one purchase in the **last 90 days** (from `CURRENT_DATE`).
    *   Made at least one purchase in the **90 days prior to the last 90 days** (i.e., between 91 and 180 days ago from `CURRENT_DATE`).
    *   Purchased products from *at least one new category* in the last 90 days that they had *never purchased before* in their entire purchasing history prior to the last 90 days.

2.  **"Loyal Niche Buyers"**: These are customers who meet *all* of the following criteria:
    *   Made at least one purchase in the **last 90 days** (from `CURRENT_DATE`).
    *   Made at least one purchase in the **90 days prior to the last 90 days** (i.e., between 91 and 180 days ago from `CURRENT_DATE`).
    *   Have *NOT* purchased any new categories in the last 90 days that they had never purchased before prior to the last 90 days.

Exclude any customers who do not meet the "consistently active" criteria (i.e., not having purchases in both time windows).

Your output should include `customer_id` and their `segment`.
