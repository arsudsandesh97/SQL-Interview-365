# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying First-Mile Bottlenecks in Logistics Shipments

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at 'SwiftRoute Logistics', a company specializing in multi-modal freight delivery. The operations team is facing challenges with consistently late deliveries and wants to improve overall efficiency. They suspect that many late arrivals are caused by significant delays occurring early in a shipment's journey, which then cascade through subsequent legs. Your task is to identify the *first* specific shipment leg (and its associated origin facility) where a significant delay *initially* occurred for any shipment that ultimately arrived late. A 'significant delay' is defined as any individual leg's actual completion time exceeding its planned completion time by more than 60 minutes.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

---


# Task
Write a SQL query to identify, for each shipment that ultimately arrived late (`final_delivery_status = 'Delivered Late'`), the *first* leg in its journey where the actual completion time exceeded its planned completion time by more than 60 minutes.

The output should include:
- `shipment_id`
- `bottleneck_leg_sequence` (the `leg_sequence` of the identified bottleneck leg)
- `bottleneck_origin_facility_id` (the `origin_facility_id` of the bottleneck leg)
- `planned_leg_end_time` (the `planned_end_time` of the bottleneck leg)
- `actual_leg_end_time` (the `actual_end_time` of the bottleneck leg)
- `delay_minutes` (the total delay in minutes for that specific leg: `actual_end_time` - `planned_end_time`)
