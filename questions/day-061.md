# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Coupon-Driven Customer Reactivation

## Business Scenario
An e-commerce company is heavily investing in promotional campaigns to re-engage dormant customers. They suspect that while many customers use coupons, not all coupon-applied orders represent truly incremental purchases from previously inactive users. The Marketing team wants to identify customers who were genuinely reactivated by a promotional coupon. Specifically, they need to find customers who had a significant period of inactivity (dormancy) and then made their *first* coupon-applied purchase, bringing them back into active shopping. This insight will help evaluate the true impact of their reactivation strategies.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Database Setup

## Create Customers Table
```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    registration_date DATE
);
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE Orders (
    order_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_id INT,
    order_date DATE,
    total_amount DECIMAL(10, 2),
    coupon_code_used VARCHAR(50), -- NULL if no coupon was used
    FOREIGN KEY (customer_id) REFERENCES Customers(customer_id)
```

# Task
Identify customers who were considered 'dormant' for at least 90 days prior to their *first purchase using a promotional coupon*. A customer is considered dormant if their most recent previous order occurred more than 90 days before the current order date, AND they have *at least one previous order* (this excludes truly new customers who have never ordered before).

For these 'reactivated by coupon' customers, determine:
1.  Their `customer_id`.
2.  The `order_id` of their *first* coupon-applied order that brought them out of dormancy.
3.  The `order_date` of that specific order.
4.  The `total_amount` of that order.
5.  The `coupon_code_used` for that order.

Sort the results by `customer_id` and then `order_date`.
