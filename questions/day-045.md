# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Inventory for Obsolescence

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at 'Global Logistics Solutions', a leading supply chain and warehousing company. Your inventory management team is facing challenges with optimizing warehouse space and reducing carrying costs. They suspect that a significant portion of their inventory consists of slow-moving items that are at risk of becoming obsolete, tying up capital and space. They need your help to identify these 'at-risk' items.

Specifically, for each unique product in each warehouse, they want to flag items where the current `quantity_on_hand` represents more than a 90-day supply, based on the `average daily sales` of that item in that warehouse over the *past 30 days*. If an item had no sales in the past 30 days, it should automatically be considered 'at-risk' due to effectively infinite days of supply.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query that returns a list of products in specific warehouses that are considered 'at-risk' for obsolescence. An item is 'at-risk' if its `quantity_on_hand` represents more than a 90-day supply based on its `average daily sales` over the last 30 days (from `CURRENT_DATE - 30 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 1 day`, inclusive). If an item had no sales in the past 30 days, its `projected_days_of_supply` should be considered effectively infinite (e.g., a very large number like 999999.00) and thus it should be flagged.

The output should include the `product_name`, `warehouse_name`, `quantity_on_hand`, the calculated `avg_daily_sales_last_30_days` (rounded to 2 decimal places), and the `projected_days_of_supply` (rounded to 2 decimal places). Order the results by `product_name` then `warehouse_name`.
