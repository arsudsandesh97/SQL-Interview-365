# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: SaaS Account Churn Risk Identification

## Business Scenario
A SaaS company offers a suite of analytical tools to its corporate clients. One of their most critical features is the "Reporting Dashboard," which provides key business insights. The company has observed that a significant drop in the usage of this specific feature often precedes customer churn. As a Data Analyst, your task is to identify accounts that show early signs of churn risk based on a substantial decline in their "Reporting Dashboard" usage. This information will be used by the customer success team for proactive outreach.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify `account_id` and `account_name` of active accounts that are at risk of churning. An account is considered "at risk" if **all** of the following conditions are met:
1.  The account had an active subscription for the **entire previous full calendar month** (September 2023, i.e., from '2023-09-01' to '2023-09-30').
2.  The total usage count of the 'Reporting Dashboard' feature for the **current partial month** (October 2023, up to `CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'`) is less than 50% of its **average monthly usage** of the same feature over the **three full calendar months immediately preceding the current month** (July, August, and September 2023).

Return the `account_id`, `account_name`, their `current_month_usage_count`, and their `previous_3_month_avg_usage_count`.
Order the results by `account_id` ascending.
